RealTime Incidents Alerts

Alerts

"Delivering Incident Details at Your Fingertips – Accessible through the Website & App"

The online real-time incident alerts provide up-to-the-minute information on emergencies, including fires, accidents, and rescue operations. These alerts are accessible via the department's official website, Agni Sandes mobile app, or social media platforms, ensuring the public stays informed and prepared. Key details such as location, incident type and response updates are shared promptly. Real-time alerts help the public avoid affected areas, assist emergency planning, and support timely decision-making. First responders and authorities use this system to enhance coordination and transparency. These platforms instantly deliver critical information, promoting safety and community resilience during emergencies.
