The Special Course in General Fire Prevention & Fire Fighting of Batch No. XXXI for Employees of Taj Group of Hotels was conducted by this Directorate w.e.f 24.01.2024 to 07.02.2024, wherein 29 Trainees from across the State of India have successfully undergone 15 days Training. The Training was comprised of both Theoretical and Practical Classes.

Shri Nitin Raiker, Director, Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services, Panaji, was the Chief Guest for the valedictory function & Shri Rajendra A. Haldankar, Dy. Director (Fire), Shri. Anmol Ahluwalia, Area Director, Operations Goa, and Shri. Savio Dmello Manager, Liaison & Administration Goa attended the function. The Director in his speech congratulated Shri. Unaiz Khan, Miss. Soujanya and Shri. Subhash Singh Kanwal for securing 1st, 2nd & 3rd place in the Theory & Practical Training assessment, he also wished the other candidates and requested them to share the training imparted to their colleagues at their respective work place and also to maintain the discipline.

During the training the trainees were subjected to various topics on Fire Prevention and Protection Measures such as Fixed Fire Fighting Installation, Evacuation Drill Procedure, Technological Advancement in the field of Fire Fighting, Disaster Management etc. The Department till date has successfully Trained 770 Trainees from Taj Group of Hotels.

Shri. Ravi H. Naik, Station Fire Officer compered the function and Shri. Bosco Ferrao, Asst. Divisional Officer, (North Zone) proposed the Vote of Thanks.

]]> 36th ANNUAL INSPECTION DAY PARADE HELD ON 16th MARCH 2024 https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/public-disclosures/events/36th-annual-inspection-day-parade-held-on-16th-march-2024-2/ Fri, 20 Jun 2025 07:44:36 +0000 https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/?post_type=cool_timeline&p=12479

The 36th Annual Inspection Day Parade of the Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services, Goa was held on 16th March 2024 at the Goa State Fire Force Training Ground, St. Inez, Panaji – Goa. The Inspection Day Parade is an annual event to test the overall operational fitness level of the Fire Personnel for prompt response in the event of any emergency. Certificates and Trophies were handed over to the winners of the Drill Competition and the Annual Sports events. Dr. V. Candavelou, I.A.S., Principal Secretary (Finance), Government of Goa was the Hon. Chief Guest for the function.

Shri Nitin V. Raiker, Director, Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services initially inspected the Annual Parade at 7:30 am and the best turn out of the Fire Personnel from various Ranks were selected for the Best Turn out Awards. The First Place for the Squad Drill was awarded to Panaji Fire Station, Ladder Drill to Fire Force Headquarters, Pump Drill to Mapusa Fire Station and Tug of War to Fire Force Headquarters. The Best Turn Out was awarded to Shri. Ganesh Govekar, Station Fire Officer, Shri. Rohidas D Parab, Shri. Sakharam Pednekar & Shri. Francisco Azavedo, Sub Officer, Shri. Bablo Majik, Shri. Gurunath Bhat Dessai, Shri. Umesh Achari & Shri. Domnic Martins, Leading Fire Fighters, Shri. Dyaneshwar Mandrekar, Driver Operator, Shri. Satish Mhamal, Shri. Paresh Mandrekar & Shri. Avinash Gaonkar, Fire Fighters and Best Platoon was awarded to Platoon No. 01 of Shri. Santosh Gawas, Station Fire Officer. Winners of the 11th Annual Sports Meet for Cricket was awarded to Central Zone, Football to South Zone & Volleyball to North Zone.

Shri. Nitin V. Raiker, Director, Fire & Emergency Services in his welcome address stated that the Annual Inspection Parade is an important occasion for the department wherein the Directorate is maintaining a healthy tradition of monthly Parades which ensure that the personnel at field level remains operationally fit and smartly Turn Out. The Annual Inspection Parade is a finishing touch to these efforts and the prizes awarded on the occasion has the salutary effect of improving the standards of performance.

Further he praised the Government for earnestly supporting the procurement of the sophisticated equipment for fire-fighting & Life Rescue in High Rise Buildings and also for handling of major disasters, as due to this technological advancements in the field of fire fighting it has resulted in better performance in saving life and property. Also further he stated that the Canacona Fire Station is presently functioning in a makeshift premises and the Department has identified the Land of Goa Housing Board, wherein the Goa Housing Board has agreed to provide a land of 2275 Sq. Mtrs and the proposal is submitted to the Government for approval to purchase a land. Shri. Nitin Raiker in his speech requested the Hon. Principal Secretary (finance) to look into the long pending issue of Fire Risk Allowance to all the Fire Personnel as per the recommendations of Central Seventh Pay Commission.

Dr. V. Candavelou, I.A.S., Principal Secretary (Finance), Government of Goa, in his Speech commended the performance of the March Past, Ladder Drill and Winners of the Athletic & Sports Events. Further he praised the department’s performance towards prompt & effective response in attending to Fire & Emergency Calls compared to the last three years. He further praised the Director, Fire & Emergency Services for his efforts in ensuring that the proposals for the upliftment of the department are obtained in a time bound manner from the Government.

The Department had organized Exhibition of Ladder Drill event which was carried out by the Fire Force Headquarters Fire Personnel crew, and the same was excellently executed within a timing of 1.33 mins and also displayed the exhibition of various equipment’s related to Fire Fighting, Search & Rescue, Swift Water/Flood Rescue, Medical First Responder (MFR), Urban Search & Rescue (USAR) and Lighting Equipment’s etc. for the general public at the Goa State Fire Force Training Ground.

Shri Ajit Kamat, Asst. Divisional Officer compered the function and proposed the Vote of Thanks.

]]> ANNUAL RAISING/FAMILY DAY FUNCTION https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/public-disclosures/events/annual-raising-family-day-function-2/ Fri, 20 Jun 2025 07:43:33 +0000 https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/?post_type=cool_timeline&p=12475

The Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services celebrated its Annual Raising/ Family Day Function on January 21, 2025 at 16:00 hrs. The event was held at the Princessa Boat Cruise, Santa Monica Jetty, Panaji and witnessed a gathering of dignitaries, staff, parents, and students. A highlight of the ceremony was about the awarding of scholarships to 110 deserving children of the Fire Personnel across the State. The event marked a significant occasion for the Directorate, showcasing its commitment to the welfare and educational growth of its community. Shri. Nitin V. Raiker, Director, Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services officiated as the Chief Guest for the function. He was joined by Shri. Pundalik A. Parab, Dy. Director (Admn.), Shri. Francisco Mendes, Divisional Officer of South Zone; Shri. Shripad Gawas, Divisional Officer of North Zone; Shri. Ajit K. Kamat, Assistant Divisional Officer of Central Zone; Shri. Bosco Ferrao, Assistant Divisional Officer of North Zone; Shri. Deepak Sawant, Honorary Secretary of the Goa Fire Service Welfare Fund; Shri. Milind Dessai, Honorary Treasurer of the Goa Fire Service Welfare Fund; and Smt. Neha Nitin Raiker. Their presence added gravitas to the event, underscoring the importance of this milestone celebration.

During his keynote address, Shri. Nitin V. Raiker expressed his heartfelt congratulations to the scholarship recipients for their accomplishments in the academic year 2023-2024. He commended their dedication and encouraged them to excel in their studies, aiming for higher achievements in the future. Shri. Raiker also advised the students to cultivate discipline, respect their parents, and embrace positive habits and thoughts. He emphasized that such values play a pivotal role in shaping a successful and fulfilling life.

Shri. Raiker also took the opportunity to highlight the significant role of the Goa Fire Service Welfare Fund. He acknowledged the fund’s consistent efforts in supporting the educational pursuits of children from financially constrained backgrounds, especially those enrolled in professional courses. He also commended the members of the Managing Committee for their exemplary dedication and meticulous efforts in orchestrating this exceptional event. He thanked Hon’ble Chief Secretary, Government of Goa, President of the Goa Fire Service Welfare Fund, Dr. V. Candavelou, IAS for giving the opportunity to celebrate the Family Day function on the Cruise Boat. Further he conveyed the best wishes of the Hon’ble Chief Secretary to all the students and their parents.

To enhance the entertainment quotient of the event, a spellbinding Cultural Program by the Tiswadi Cultural Sangh, Curca, and a lively Karaoke Music session by Dada Music, Kumbarjhua, were organized for the enjoyment of children and families. The captivating one-hour performances were brimming with energy, talent, and cultural vibrancy, leaving the audience thoroughly engaged and entertained. Both children and parents were delighted by the exceptional displays, which added a distinctive charm to the celebration. The seamless blend of cultural artistry and musical melodies made the event a resounding success, fostering a sense of joy and community among the attendees.

The Annual Raising/family Day Function was a resounding success, celebrating not only the academic achievements of young students but also reinforcing the Directorate’s dedication to community welfare and support. The event served as a platform to recognize the hard work of the Fire & Emergency Services personnel and their families while providing an entertaining and enriching experience for all attendees.

Shri. Bosco Ferrao, Asst. Divisional Officer, North Zone presented the Welcome Address.     Shri. Ravi Naik, Station Fire Officer compered the function. Shri. Naik also delivered a heartfelt vote of thanks, expressing gratitude to everyone who contributed to the success of the Raising/Family Day Function.

]]> NATIONAL FIRE SERVICE DAY 14th APRIL 2024 https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/public-disclosures/events/national-fire-service-day-14th-april-2024-2/ Fri, 20 Jun 2025 07:42:30 +0000 https://milroyg.github.io/dfes/?post_type=cool_timeline&p=12470

The National Fire Service Day was observed on Sunday 14th April 2024 which was organized by the Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services in collaboration with the Office of the Chief Electoral Officer. This year the National Theme for the fire prevention week was.

“Ensure Fire Safety, Contribute towards Nation Building”

“अग्नि सुरक्षा सुनिश्चित करें, राष्ट्रीय निर्माण में योगदान दें”

On 14th April 2024, wreaths were laid at the “Martyrs Memorial Column” at the Fire Force Training Ground, Panaji by Shri Sanjit Rodrigues, IAS, Secretary, Govt. of Goa, Shri. Nitin V. Raiker, Director, Fire & Emergency Services & all the Ranks from Deputy Director (Fire) to Fire Fighter. A signature campaign was undertaken at the training ground as a SVEEP campaign. Two minutes silence was observed by all the Officials present in memory of Firemen Martyrs who laid down their lives while on duty. The Director, Fire & Emergency Services explained the significance of the observance of the National Fire Service Day and read out the names of fireman martyrs who laid down their lives while on duty.

The Chief Guest for the function Shri Sanjit Rodrigues, IAS, reviewed the CEREMONIAL PARADE. Commendation Certificates for exemplary performance in the field of Rescue Operation, Good Discipline & General Behavior and Devotion towards Duties alongwith the Best Fire Fighter in good discipline & general behavior were also awarded to the various ranks of the departmental operational fire personnel at the hands of Shri Sanjit Rodrigues, IAS, Secretary, Govt. of Goa and Shri. Nitin V. Raiker, Director, Fire & Emergency Services.

Shri Nitin V Raiker in his welcome address stated that the National Fire Service Day is followed by Fire Prevention Week from 14th till 20th April 2024. The theme for this year is “Ensure Fire Safety, Contribute towards Nation Building”. During the Fire Prevention Week, various Fire Safety awareness programme have been arranged for the public in the State of Goa. The public are requested to acquire knowledge through this awareness programme so that they can help the people in saving life and property of the Country. Further in his speech Shri. Raiker informed that inspite of all the precautions, fire incidents do occur, either through human error or through accident. During the period 2023-24, the Fire & Emergency Services have collectively attended 8000 Fire & Emergency Calls (3000 Fire and 5000 Emergency) during which 194 human lives and 757 Animal lives were saved. Property worth of Rs.179 Crores has been saved due to timely action of the Fire Personnel. As a part of this awareness campaign, a Poster Competition was organized by the Directorate of Fire & Emergency Services in collaboration with the Office of the Chief Electoral Officer for the Primary and Middle School Children of the State on 06th April 2024 wherein a total of 2000 students from schools all over the state participated in the Poster Competition. The Competition was divided into two groups, Group-I (7-10 years) – Topic: “Fire Friend & Fire Foe” and Group-II (11-14 years) – Topic: “Environmental Hazard due to Fire”. Prizes were awarded to those students who stood 1st, 2nd and 3rd in the Poster Competition in both Groups on 14th April 2024, National Fire Service Day, at the hands of Shri Sanjit Rodrigues.

Shri Sanjit Rodrigues, in his speech joined the Fire Professionals in paying homage to the Martyrs of this noble profession in India who have laid down their lives in their mission to save other lives. Their dedication to duty will inspire generations of Citizens. He further stated that the Nation and our State have been making steady progress on the Economic and Social Development Front. Unfortunately, Fire takes a toll on these gains we make. Lives, which could have continued to contribute to the growth, are lost. Properties worth Crores of Rupees are lost. For this reason, it is noteworthy that the country has decided to focus on preventing Fires to protect the development we achieve. He stressed that there is a need to ensure that this year’s national Fire Service Day theme message reaches all the citizens. He lauded the efforts of all the fire personnel and commended the Department for their disciplined and effective services to the State.

Further Pledge was also administered “Noting like Voting, I Vote for Sure” by the dignitaries, fire officials and other attendees present at the function.

The Department had organized Live demonstrations of Fire Fighting and Rescue operation for the public and displayed the exhibition of various equipment’s related to Fire Fighting, Search & Rescue, Swift Water/Flood Rescue, Medical First Responder (MFR), Urban Search & Rescue (USAR) and Lighting Equipment’s for the general public at the Goa State Fire Force Training Ground.

Shri Ajit K. Kamat, Asst. Divisional Officer, Fire Force Headquarters compered the function & proposed the Vote of Thanks.
